## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Funding flow for disputed costs
For each disputed cost line the system SHALL guide:
1. Understand the cost.
2. Check affordability.
3. Agree the bank funding plan (bank loan + escrow of the seller's likely share); the bank pack, client consent + PwC independence, bank questions and milestone-based releases follow.
4. Settle the dispute using the SPA reading and an agreed seller share.
5. Record an anonymised lesson.

Nothing SHALL reach the bank without client consent.
